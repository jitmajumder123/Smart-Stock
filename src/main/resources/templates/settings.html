Stock monitoring

Each product has its own reorder point. The dashboard flags products when available stock reaches or falls below that value.

Update reorder pointsEdit a product in Products and set its reorder point.
Record stock received or removedUse Inventory → Adjust stock. Each adjustment is written to movement history.
Order reservation policyPending, Processing, and Completed orders reserve stock. Cancelling or deleting an order returns its reserved units.
Revenue reportingOnly Completed orders count as revenue. Reports can be filtered by month and year.
Audit historyReview recent manual adjustments and order movements from Inventory.
